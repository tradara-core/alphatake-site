How it works

From one sentence to a portfolio you can track.

  1. Step 1 · Write your view

    Say what you believe will happen.

  2. Step 2 · See what you would own

    The engine builds the portfolio.

    It considers every company listed on the NSE that trades at least ₹5 crore a day, scores each one by how much your view moves it, allows for what the market already expects, and sets how much goes into each.

    • Companies and funds, each with its share and the reason it is there.
    • Whole shares at real prices. What stays as cash is shown to the paisa.
  3. Step 3 · Invest through your broker

    Approve once. The orders go to your broker.

    Connect the broker you already use, review every order, and approve them together. Your money and your shares stay in your own account. AlphaTake never holds either.

    • Every order is shown before it is placed.
    • Until then, practise with ₹10 lakh of paper money at real prices.

  4. Step 4 · Track it every trading day

    Know whether your view is working.

    Each take watches the signs that would show it is right or wrong, and tells you when one changes. You see what moved it today and the news on every company it holds.

    • Every take is measured against the Nifty 50 over the same days.
    • Each week the engine runs your take again. If the companies or weights change, you approve the trades first.

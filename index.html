  2. II · See what you would own

    About 400 companies, weighed against one belief.

    AlphaTake reads the sources, scores the market against your view, and builds the portfolio: which companies, how much of each, funds where they fit, and the reason each one is there.

    • Whole shares at real prices, with what stays as cash shown to the paisa.
    • Change anything before you go ahead.
  3. III · Invest through your own broker

    One approval. Your broker, your account.

    When you are ready, the orders go to the broker you already use. AlphaTake never holds your money, and nothing is placed without your approval.

    • Every order is shown before it is placed.
    • The shares are yours, in your own demat account.

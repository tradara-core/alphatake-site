How it works

From a hunch to a portfolio, on your phone

    Everyone has an opinion on the market. Almost no one checks if they were right. AlphaTake turns every opinion into a portfolio, and every portfolio into a score.

    Built to tell you if you were right

    Paper money, real market

    Buys happen at real prices, less the charges a broker would take. Your money is never touched, and your record never resets.

    Scored against the Nifty 50

    Every take is measured against the index over the same trading days. You see straight away if your view beat the market.

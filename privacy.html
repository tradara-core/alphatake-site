AlphaTake

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

AlphaTake is an app where you write a view about the market and see how it would have done with paper money. It is run by Tradara Technologies Private Limited ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your name and email address, and an ID from Apple or Google if you sign in with them. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we only see Apple's relay address.
  • Profile. The display name, handle and photo you choose.
  • What you make in the app. Your takes (the views you write), the paper portfolios built from them, their paper trades and results, the people you follow, and the takes you agree or disagree with.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, we receive a technical report about the error and your device model. These reports never include your take text or amounts.

We do not collect your contacts, location, real brokerage accounts or real money. AlphaTake uses paper money only.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To turn your takes into paper portfolios and track how they perform.
  • To show your public profile and takes to other AlphaTake users, and theirs to you.
  • To send you sign-in codes by email.
  • To find and fix bugs.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

What other people can see

Your profile and public takes are visible to other signed-in AlphaTake users, along with each take's performance. Holdings in other people's portfolios are shown as whole percentages only, never as amounts. Takes you keep private are visible only to you.

Who processes it for us

  • Amazon Web Services runs our servers, and runs the AI models that read your takes and build portfolios (Amazon Bedrock).
  • Supabase hosts our database.
  • Resend sends sign-in code emails.
  • Sentry receives crash reports.
  • Apple and Google confirm your identity when you sign in with them.

These providers process data on our behalf and only for these purposes. Some of them may store data outside India.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. Sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, takes, portfolios, follows and sign-in links. Where someone else built on one of your takes, their copy stays but no longer points to you.

Your choices

  • Delete your account at any time in the app, from your profile's settings. If you signed in with Apple, we also tell Apple to disconnect AlphaTake from your Apple ID.
  • Access or correct your data, or ask a question about it, by writing to us at the address below.

We handle your data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

AlphaTake is for people 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Sign-in codes are stored only as hashes, and Apple sign-in tokens are encrypted at rest.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
